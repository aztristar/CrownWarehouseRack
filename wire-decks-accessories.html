New & Used Wire Decks for Pallet Rack Systems

Wire decks are the most common shelving solution for teardrop-style pallet racks. They sit on top of step beams or channel beams and provide a solid, air-permeable surface for pallet and box storage. Crown Warehouse Rack stocks new and used wire decks in all standard sizes ready for immediate delivery.

Two Types Available

Flared Type vs. Flush Type Wire Decks

Flared Type (Step Beam)

Flared wire decks have bent-up flanges at the front and back that hook over the top of step beams. This is the most common type and works on standard teardrop-style step beams. The flanges keep the deck securely in place.

  • Hooks over step beams — no hardware needed
  • Most common type — widest availability
  • Works on Interlake, Ridg-U-Rak, and similar step beams
  • New and used both in stock

Flush Type (Channel Beam / U-Brace)

Flush wire decks sit flat on channel beams and are supported by U-shaped wire braces underneath. These are used on channel-beam rack systems and provide a flat, flush surface when installed.

  • Sits on channel beams with U-brace supports
  • Flush top surface when installed
  • Compatible with channel beam rack systems
  • Ready to ship — in stock
