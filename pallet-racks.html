What We Carry

Heavy-Duty Pallet Rack Systems — New & Used

Crown Warehouse Rack carries one of the largest selections of new and used pallet rack components in Southern California. Whether you need a single bay or a full warehouse system, we have the inventory and the expertise to get your project done fast.

We specialize in Interlake-style teardrop uprights and beams — the most common and interchangeable pallet rack system in the industry. New beams and uprights in multiple sizes are in stock at both our Rancho Cucamonga and Riverside locations.

  • Teardrop-style uprights: 16 ft, 20 ft, 22 ft, 24 ft, 26 ft, 28 ft heights in stock
  • Upright depths: 36", 42", 48" standard
  • 96" (8 ft) and 144" (12 ft) load beams — step beams and channel beams
  • 2", 3", 4", and 5" step beam profiles available
  • New and old style Interlake/teardrop connections
  • Home Depot style racking compatible
  • High-pile storage rack systems for 20–28 ft clear height applications
