City Rack Permits

Full-Service City Rack Permit Assistance

California cities require permits for high-pile storage installations. The process involves multiple agencies, engineering certifications, and detailed documentation. Crown Warehouse Rack handles the entire permit process for you — from the first layout drawing to the final inspection sign-off.

📄

Formal Rack Layouts

Detailed floor plan drawings of your rack system showing aisle widths, rack heights, bay configurations, and compliance details.

🏛

City & Fire Department Liaison

We work directly with the Building Department and Fire Department on your behalf — managing submittals, responses, and corrections.

⚙

Seismic Calculations

Certified structural engineers provide seismic calculations and stamped drawing packages required by California building codes.

🔧

Anchor Bolt Inspection

Anchor bolt inspections by certified inspectors to verify code-compliant base-plate and anchor installations.

🔥

High-Pile Storage Reports

High-pile storage reports and fire consultant coordination for warehouses storing commodities above 12 ft in California.

⚖

Core Testing & ADA

Core testing of concrete floors, ADA compliance expertise, and complete blueprints and permit drawing packages.
