Two Types Available

Roll-Formed & Structural I-Beam Cantilever Racks

Roll-Formed Cantilever (Standard)

Roll-formed cantilever racks are the most common and economical option for lighter to medium-duty applications. These are available as single-sided or double-sided starter sections and add-on sections. Many different sizes available.

Ideal for: retail building materials, PVC pipe, lightweight lumber, plastic sheet goods, and general-purpose long-item storage.

  • Single-sided and double-sided configurations
  • Multiple arm lengths and capacities
  • Tapered incline arms available
  • X-bracing up to 10 ft wide bays
  • Many sizes in stock — call for current inventory

Structural I-Beam Cantilever (Heavy Duty)

Structural cantilever racks use heavy-duty I-beam columns and arms for demanding industrial applications. These are designed for high capacity loads and long spans. We carry both single-sided and double-sided structural cantilever sections — including large runs of 25+ bays.

Ideal for: heavy lumber, steel bar stock, pipe, structural steel, aluminum extrusions, particle board, and long pallets.

  • Structural I-beam columns — extremely heavy duty
  • 7 ft picking height to 16 ft+ high columns in stock
  • High-pile double-sided configurations
  • Long runs of 25+ bay sections available
  • In stock at Riverside County location
